Frequently Asked Questions

Are all watches covered by an official warranty in India?

Yes. Every mechanical and quartz timepiece includes a 5-year movement warranty serviced at our Mumbai and Bengaluru ateliers.

How is high-value shipping insured?

All orders ship via BlueDart Air Vault in tamper-evident packaging with 100% transit insurance and OTP-verified handover.

Shipping & Insured Delivery Policy

Complimentary insured express air shipping across 19,000+ Indian PIN codes. Metro orders arrive within 24–48 hours; rest of India within 3–5 business days.

14-Day Return & Refund Policy

Unworn watches with intact protective seals, tags, and presentation box may be returned within 14 days for a full refund to your original Razorpay payment method within 5–7 business days.

5-Year International Movement Warranty

Covers manufacturing defects in the movement, hands, and dial for 60 months from the invoice date. Normal strap wear and accidental crystal impact are excluded.
